## Research Paper Annotations General Guidelines

### Underlines and Highlights

#### Underlines

- Blue Underline: Methods, Techniques and Related; with outcome mentioned
	or easily inferred

- Orange Underline: Results, Outcomes and Findings, and Related

- Magenta Underline: Methods, Techniques and Related; outcome or purpose
	not easily inferred and method needs to be learnt

- Red Underline: Statement or claim that needs to be verified, understood
	or inferred

- Green Underline: SINGLE WORD, PHRASE OR SMALL COLLECTION THEREOF. Key word to be focused for precise and accurate
	inference. The sentence may be inferred otherways without focus on
	this word

- Yellow Underline: STATEMENT, CLAIM, FINDING, OUTCOME or RELATED that needs to
	be looked, addressed, noted or  inferred.


### Highlights

- Yellow highlight: Key word to be focused for precise and accurate
	inference. The sentence may be inferred otherways without focus on
	this word


-----

NB: The Underlines and Highlights usage may be mixed, but currently they are viewed and used as being mutually 
	exclusive. Thus an overlap and redundancy may be observed among the two.
