# SERUM

**Systematic Evidence review and Research Understanding Methodology**

SERUM is a personal and evolving framework for Research-oriented Learning (RoL), literature review, scientific reading, annotation, and knowledge extraction from research publications.

The goal of this repository is to develop and document practical methods for:

* Reading and understanding research papers efficiently
* Systematic annotation and note-taking
* Evidence extraction and synthesis
* Literature review workflows
* Research methodology learning
* Knowledge organization and retention

## Current Contents

* Research paper annotation guidelines
* Annotation color-coding conventions
* Reading and inference workflows

## Intended Audience

This repository is primarily designed for students, self-learners, and researchers who wish to develop a structured approach toward reading and understanding scientific literature.

## Status

SERUM is currently under active development and will be expanded as new methods, workflows, and best practices are refined through practical use.

## Contributing

At present, this repository serves as a personal knowledge and methodology archive. Suggestions, discussions, and constructive feedback are welcome.
